RICE-GOVGovernment EditionIn development

One ecosystem. Re-benchmarked for public trust.

Same 9 pillars, same maturity model, same citation discipline as RICE-FSI — rebuilt around a structural reality unique to government: Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act doesn't legally bind the federal government at all. RICE-GOV exists to hold citizen-data systems to the standard the law hasn't yet mandated.

§ 00 · WHY A SEPARATE EDITION

The accountability gap, made structural.

Government-held citizen data carries the highest concentration risk of any sector — and currently, the least legal obligation to protect it.

01

PDPA doesn't apply to government

Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 excludes federal and state government entities entirely, and doesn't cover non-commercial transactions either. Systems like PADU, MySejahtera, and MyJPJ operate outside the accountability regime private companies face.

02

Consolidation without proportionate control

Malaysia's digital government strategy is actively merging IC numbers, biometrics, income data, and health records into interconnected "super app" architecture — MyDigital ID alone is designed to bridge PADU, MySejahtera, MyJPJ, MyGov, and LHDN.

03

The record shows the pattern, not the headline

A widely-circulated "17 million MyKad leak" claim was investigated and refuted by NRD/NACSA in December 2024 — the samples were recycled 2015–2017 data. But the Auditor-General's own reports document real access-governance failures underneath the noise, which is what RICE-GOV benchmarks against.

§ 01 · WHAT'S ON THE RECORD

Confirmed, not rumoured.

RICE-GOV is built only against verified findings — official audit reports and confirmed incidents, not unverified dark-web claims.

MySejahtera — Auditor-General 2021

A third-party-held "Super Admin" account downloaded 3 million vaccine recipients' data across multiple IPs over four days — not revoked until two days after the final download. 1.12 million intrusion attempts were also logged against the same database.

PADU — public trust deficit

CyberSecurity Malaysia had to issue public assurances of PADU's security in March 2024. Registration stalled at 9.24M of a 22M target — security and privacy concerns were a documented factor.

Confirmed breach pattern, 2022–2025

Election Commission, Maybank, and Astro data theft (2022); SOCSO breach of MyKad numbers, salaries, and addresses (2023); Prasarana Malaysia ransomware, 300GB leaked (Aug 2024); a hacker's claim of breaching 12+ government websites (Aug 2025).

Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854)

Government is one of the 11 statutory NCII sectors. Act 854 mandates incident reporting and audits — but doesn't fill the PDPA accountability gap for how citizen data is handled day to day.

§ 02 · STATUS
