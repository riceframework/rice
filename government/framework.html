P1DRAFT

Governance & Risk Oversight

DATA CONSOLIDATION RISK · CITIZEN TRUST · CROSS-AGENCY ACCOUNTABILITY

The foundation pillar — but in government, risk appetite has to answer for citizen trust and legal accountability that the law itself hasn't fully assigned yet. Draft v0.1, benchmarked against Cybersecurity Act 2024 and confirmed Auditor-General findings.

Establish a Government Digital Security Governance framework with named ministerial or agency-head accountability — not diffuse ownership across vendors and departments

Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854)◆ RICE-GOV

Named-accountability requirement — MySejahtera's Auditor-General findings show what happens when ownership of a citizen-facing system is unclear: a third-party-held privileged account went unmonitored for days

Define risk appetite weighted by citizen-trust and social-stability impact, not system uptime alone

◆ RICE-GOV

Trust-weighted risk scoring — adapted from RICE-HC's patient-safety weighting; for government, the equivalent harm is erosion of public confidence in digital services

Voluntarily apply PDPA-equivalent data protection obligations to all citizen-facing systems, pending legal reform closing the government exemption

◆ RICE-GOV

The core gap-fill objective — Malaysia's PDPA 2010 does not legally bind federal or state government entities. PADU, MySejahtera, and MyJPJ currently operate outside the accountability regime a private company would face for the same data.

Eliminate standing privileged-access accounts; require time-bound, logged access grants with mandatory revocation within 24 hours of task completion

Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854)◆ RICE-GOV

Directly responsive to the confirmed 2021 Auditor-General finding: a third-party "Super Admin" account on MySejahtera downloaded 3 million records over four days and wasn't revoked until two days after the final download

Formalise cross-agency data-sharing agreements with mandatory access logging for any system drawing from national-database-class infrastructure

◆ RICE-GOV

Cross-agency governance — Malaysia's digital ID strategy is actively consolidating PADU, MySejahtera, MyJPJ, MyGov, and LHDN; no framework currently governs the access boundaries between them

Commission independent security assessment and publish a public transparency summary before any major citizen-facing system launch

◆ RICE-GOV

Proactive trust-building, not reactive reassurance — PADU's adoption stalled at 9.24M of a 22M target partly over unaddressed public security concerns; CyberSecurity Malaysia's March 2024 statement came only after public pressure

Comply with Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854) NCII obligations for the Government sector — incident notification, risk assessment, and audit cadence

Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854)

Align RICE maturity model scoring to existing public-accountability reporting cadence — Auditor-General and Parliamentary review cycles

◆ RICE-GOV

RICE Maturity Model, mapped to a mechanism that already exists and already works — the Auditor-General's reports are how MySejahtera's failures actually came to light

Address administration and political-cycle rotation risk — maintain institutional security memory across minister and agency-head transitions

◆ RICE-GOV

Rotation-resilient governance, adapted from RICE-FSI P1.08 — government leadership turnover happens on an electoral cycle, an even shorter horizon than corporate C-suite rotation

Key Controls (draft)

  • Named accountable owner per citizen-facing system, published, not just internally documented
  • Privileged-access register with mandatory expiry — zero standing "Super Admin" style accounts
  • Cross-agency data-sharing agreement template with logged access boundaries
  • Pre-launch independent security assessment + public transparency summary
  • RICE-GOV Maturity Model scoring aligned to Auditor-General reporting cycle

What RICE-GOV benchmarks against (draft)

  • Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854) — Government NCII sector obligations
  • Auditor-General of Malaysia annual reports — the working precedent for what public accountability actually looks like
  • PDPA 2010 — cited for its scope gap, not its protections, since it doesn't bind government
P2DRAFT

Threat Detection & Intelligence

NACSA ADVISORIES · SUPER-APP MONITORING · CROSS-AGENCY INTEL

Draft — detection has to span a consolidating "super app" architecture, not a single agency's perimeter.

Integrate NACSA threat advisories into agency-level monitoring

Deploy centralised logging across citizen-facing super-app components — PADU, MySejahtera, MyJPJ integration layer

◆ RICE

Centralised logging across consolidated systems — the current architecture links agencies without a shared visibility layer to match

Monitor for anomalous bulk data access, directly responsive to the confirmed MySejahtera Super Admin finding

◆ RICE

A direct response to the 2021 Auditor-General finding — bulk downloads by privileged accounts should trigger real-time alerting, not be discovered years later in an audit

Establish an inter-agency threat intelligence sharing protocol

Monitor for credential-stuffing and account-takeover attempts against citizen-facing portals

P3DRAFT

Identity & Access Governance

ZERO STANDING ACCESS · MYDIGITAL ID · CIVIL SERVANT MFA

Draft — identity governance closes the exact gap the Auditor-General already found.

Eliminate standing privileged accounts across all citizen-data systems

◆ RICE

Ties directly to P1.04 — the finding that created this pillar in the first place

Integrate MyDigital ID as the standard citizen authentication layer, deprecating fragmented per-agency logins

◆ RICE

Consolidated citizen authentication — fewer, better-governed identity systems beat many loosely-secured ones

Enforce MFA for all civil-servant access to citizen-data systems

Time-bound and log all third-party vendor access to government databases

Conduct quarterly access reviews for any system holding IC-number-linked data

P4DRAFT

Incident Response & Resilience

ACT 854 NOTIFICATION · CROSS-AGENCY BREACH COORDINATION · PUBLIC DISCLOSURE

Draft — a breach in one linked system erodes trust in all of them, so coordination and disclosure discipline matter as much as containment.

Establish an incident response plan meeting Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854) notification timelines

Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854)

Define a cross-agency breach coordination protocol — a breach in one system affects trust in every linked system

◆ RICE

Trust is shared infrastructure across PADU/MySejahtera/MyJPJ-class systems even when the databases aren't

Conduct an annual ransomware tabletop exercise for critical citizen-service systems

Establish a proactive public communication protocol for confirmed incidents

◆ RICE

Proactive disclosure discipline — the pattern seen in past incidents is delayed or defensive response; RICE-GOV treats fast, honest disclosure as the control

Define RTO/RPO for citizen-facing services weighted by social-disruption impact

P5DRAFT

Data Security & Privacy

PDPA-EQUIVALENT CONTROLS · DATA CLASSIFICATION · DPIA

Draft — this pillar is where the PDPA gap becomes operational, not just a governance statement.

Apply PDPA-equivalent encryption controls voluntarily across all citizen-data systems, at rest and in transit

◆ RICE

Operationalises P1.03 — the voluntary-compliance commitment has to show up as an actual technical control, not just a policy statement

Classify data across consolidated systems — PADU, MySejahtera, MyJPJ — by sensitivity tier

Restrict biometric data storage and access to the minimum number of systems necessary

Define a data retention and deletion schedule for citizen records, avoiding indefinite retention by default

Conduct a Data Protection Impact Assessment before any new cross-agency data-sharing initiative

◆ RICE

DPIA as a gating step — the kind of upfront assessment PADU's rollout would have benefited from before public trust became the story

P6DRAFT

Supply Chain & Third-Party Risk

NACSA LICENSING · COMPETITIVE TENDER · THIRD-PARTY MANAGED SYSTEMS

Draft — procurement discipline is itself a security control when a single vendor arrangement can become a national talking point.

Require NACSA-licensed vendors for all cybersecurity services engaged

Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854)

Formalise contractual security requirements for third-party-managed citizen systems

◆ RICE

Precedent: MySejahtera's third-party management arrangement drew parliamentary scrutiny over both procurement process and security accountability

Mandate open, competitive tender for security-critical system vendors, avoiding sole-source appointments without security review

◆ RICE

Procurement-as-governance — how a vendor is selected is itself a security control when the system will hold tens of millions of citizens' data

Establish a vendor offboarding procedure with full credential and access revocation

Require an SBOM for critical citizen-facing application vendors

P7DRAFT

Infrastructure Best Practices

NCII HARDENING · ZERO TRUST INTER-AGENCY · PATCH SLA

Draft — infrastructure controls for systems legally designated as national critical infrastructure.

Harden national-critical-infrastructure systems per Act 854 NCII requirements

Cybersecurity Act 2024 (Act 854)

Segment citizen-facing systems from internal government administrative networks

Apply zero-trust network architecture for inter-agency data connections

◆ RICE

Zero-trust between agencies, not just within them — the connective tissue of the super-app architecture is where trust currently is assumed rather than verified

Maintain a patch management SLA for internet-facing government systems

Define certificate lifecycle management for inter-agency API integrations

P8DRAFT

Secure Application Ecosystem

SECURE SDLC · PRE-LAUNCH PENTEST · CROSS-SYSTEM API TESTING

Draft — application security discipline applied before launch, not discovered after.

Apply secure SDLC to all citizen-facing application development, in-house and outsourced

Conduct independent penetration testing before major system launches

◆ RICE

Ties to P1.06 — pre-launch assessment as standard practice, not a reactive measure taken only after public pressure

Harden authentication for all citizen-facing portals against credential-stuffing attacks

Test cross-system API integrations — PADU/MySejahtera/MyJPJ-class connections — for authorization flaws

◆ RICE

The integration points between consolidated systems are exactly where an authorization flaw would have the widest blast radius

Conduct regular vulnerability scanning of public-facing government web assets

P9DRAFT

Emerging Threat Horizon

AI DISINFORMATION · DEEPFAKES · POST-QUANTUM NATIONAL ID

Draft — the frontier risks for government are as much about information integrity as system security.

Assess AI-driven disinformation risk targeting government communication channels

◆ RICE

Disinformation-readiness as a security objective — a government's information channels are now an attack surface in their own right

Begin post-quantum cryptography migration planning for long-lived national identity data

◆ RICE

National identity data — MyKad, biometrics — is relevant for decades; "harvest now, decrypt later" risk applies at national scale

Monitor deepfake risk in the context of official government communications and public figures

Assess OT/cyber-physical risk for critical infrastructure under Act 854 NCII designation

Establish an AI governance framework for any government use of generative AI in citizen services
