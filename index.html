§ 04 · INTRODUCING RICE

Not a checklist. An ecosystem.

Every compliance framework tells part of the story. RICE was built from years of watching the same blind spots recur across FSI engagements — and naming the gap no single framework fills.

R

Resilient

Designed to maintain operations under attack — not just prevent breaches, but ensure continuity when they occur. Resilience is the outcome; all nine pillars contribute to it.

I

Integrated

All nine pillars work as a connected ecosystem. Controls in one pillar reinforce every other. Detection feeds response. Governance governs all. No silo security.

C

Cybersecurity

End-to-end coverage — from campus infrastructure and identity to application security, AI threats, and post-quantum cryptography migration.

E

Ecosystem

Not a checklist. A living framework built for community adoption, continuous evolution, and real-world operational context. An opening argument, not a closed document.
