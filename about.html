MJ Lee is a Senior Pre-Sales Technical Consultant based in Kuala Lumpur, with over ten years in enterprise networking and cybersecurity — spanning campus and datacentre networking, NGFW, EDR/XDR, SIEM, NDR, IAM/PAM, and cloud security. His account portfolio covers Tier-1 and Tier-2 Malaysian FSI, insurance, private tertiary education, and oil & gas manufacturing.

Across sixty-plus enterprise RFPs, his work has contributed over RM 10 million in solution revenue, with a proposal win rate above fifty percent. In FY2025 he was recognised as a Fortinet Top Pre-Sales Consultant — one of three recipients across Malaysia's partner network.

RICE grew out of a recurring observation: the same operational blind spots — OT convergence, AI-era threats, quantum readiness, vendor-bounded advice — appearing across otherwise compliant institutions. Rather than let the gap stay unnamed, he built the framework to fill it: vendor-agnostic, FSI-first, and mapped to the regulations Malaysian institutions actually answer to.

RICE v1.0 is the FSI Edition. Healthcare and Government & Critical Infrastructure adaptations are in development, and the framework is open to community contribution under #RICEFrameworkMY.
