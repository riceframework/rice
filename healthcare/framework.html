P1

Governance & Risk Oversight

CLINICAL-RISK-WEIGHTED · BOARD ACCOUNTABILITY · PATIENT SAFETY

The foundation pillar — but in healthcare, risk appetite has to be weighed against patient safety, not just financial exposure. Draft v0.1, benchmarked against HIPAA, HITRUST, and the Malaysian PDPA/APHM gap.

Establish a Health Information Security Governance framework with joint clinical-leadership and board accountability

HIPAA §164.308(a)(1)HITRUST 00.a◆ RICE-HC

Joint clinical + IT governance sign-off — existing frameworks assign accountability to IT/compliance alone, not clinical leadership

Define risk appetite weighted by patient-safety impact, not financial exposure alone

HIPAA §164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)HITRUST 03.a◆ RICE-HC

Patient-safety-weighted risk scoring — no existing framework ties cyber risk appetite to clinical harm severity

Implement continuous compliance monitoring beyond the annual HIPAA risk analysis cycle

HIPAA §164.308(a)(8)HITRUST 03.b

Mandate cybersecurity and patient-data-handling literacy training for clinical and administrative staff annually

HIPAA §164.308(a)(5)HITRUST 01.b

Establish a Digital Health Risk Committee with escalation to Medical Director and Board

HITRUST 00.a◆ RICE-HC

Bridges clinical and IT governance — a structural gap HITRUST references but doesn't mandate

Align RICE-HC maturity model scoring to board and clinical-governance reporting — Levels 1–5 per pillar

◆ RICE-HC

RICE Maturity Model, carried over unchanged from RICE-FSI — the one element that stays identical across every sector

Map technology risk to regulatory obligations: PDPA sensitive-data provisions, APHM code of practice, and pending Malaysian Cybersecurity Bill requirements

PDPA (sensitive data)◆ RICE-HC

Interim gap-fill mapping — Malaysia currently has no dedicated digital-health security law; MOH's own digital health lead has confirmed the absence of a mandated standard

Address clinical and IT leadership rotation risk — maintain institutional security memory across Medical Director / CISO transitions

◆ RICE-HC

Rotation-resilient governance, adapted from RICE-FSI P1.08 — healthcare leadership turnover carries the same institutional-memory risk

Key Controls (draft)

  • Board- and Medical-Director-approved Digital Health Security Strategy, reviewed annually
  • Clinical Risk Register — patient-safety impact, likelihood, containment owner
  • RICE-HC Maturity Model scoring per pillar (1 = Aware → 5 = Optimising)
  • Regulatory mapping: PDPA sensitive-data provisions, APHM CoP, HIPAA where cross-border data applies
  • Annual clinical-leadership + board cybersecurity simulation exercise

Vendor Alignment (draft)

  • Healthcare GRC: HITRUST MyCSF, Compliancy Group, Censinet, Clearwater
  • Clinical risk platforms: Protenus, CynergisTek — patient-data access analytics
  • Training: KnowBe4 Healthcare, HIPAA Exams — clinical-staff-specific modules
