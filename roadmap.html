Foundation. Integration. Optimisation.

A three-phase approach from current posture to RICE Level 3 baseline — BNM RMiT compliant, consistently controlled, and measurably maturing — in 12 to 18 months.

§ 07.1 · TARGET STATE

Where the roadmap lands you.

Level 3 — Defined — is the RICE baseline: BNM RMiT fully compliant, standardised controls, consistent SOC coverage. Phases beyond push priority pillars toward Managed and Optimising.

L1Aware

Ad-hoc and reactive. No formal cybersecurity programme. Incidents handled case-by-case. BNM RMiT compliance not demonstrated.

L2Developing

Policies documented. Controls inconsistently applied. Annual audits happening. Basic BNM RMiT compliance partially met.

L3DefinedBNM RMiT compliant · RICE baseline

Standardised controls in place. BNM RMiT fully compliant. NIST CSF and ISO 27001 aligned. Consistent SOC coverage.

L4Managed

Quantified risk management. Automated controls and dashboards. Proactive threat hunting. Continuous compliance posture monitoring.

L5Optimising

Predictive and adaptive. AI-augmented detection. PQC migration underway. Community contribution to RICE. Industry benchmark.
