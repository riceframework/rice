SECTOR EDITIONS

One system. Many sectors.

The 9 pillars and the L1–L5 maturity model never change — that's the architecture. What changes underneath is the benchmark: each edition is re-cut against the standards, regulations, and real operating risks specific to its sector. Same discipline, different battlefield.

GROUNDED IN LAW

Malaysia's Cyber Security Act 2024 sets the map.

Act 854 (in force since 26 August 2024) legally designates 11 National Critical Information Infrastructure sectors, with penalties up to RM500,000 and 10 years' imprisonment for non-compliance. It layers on top of existing sector regulation rather than replacing it — a bank now answers to BNM RMiT and Act 854 simultaneously. RICE's sector roadmap is mapped against this list, plus deliberate gap-fills where no regulation exists yet.

HOW A CONTROL ID READS

Citations are always edition-qualified.

Because the same pillar number can mean a different objective per sector, every citable ID carries its edition — RICE-FSI P5.03 and RICE-HC P5.03 are two different objectives, on purpose. Copy any control ID from either framework page and the edition tag comes with it.
